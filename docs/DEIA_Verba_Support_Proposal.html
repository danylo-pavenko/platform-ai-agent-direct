Problem

Support is split across four
places. None of them talk.

A Verba customer does not pick one official queue. They write wherever the brand is visible. Today that is four disconnected surfaces, each with a different clock and a different person answering.

Instagram Direct

The public brand channel. Bugs, “how do I add a font”, “I was charged twice”, and “refund my yearly plan” land in DM. A late or inconsistent reply becomes a story, a comment, or a 1-star review. The 4.9 rating is an asset at risk.

Email · support@verba.digital

App Store listings and receipts point here. Threads sit in a mailbox, outside Instagram history. The same person may already have written in Direct. Nobody sees both threads as one customer.

Stripe Dashboard inbox

Refund requests and customer messages from hosted receipts and invoices live in Stripe. There is no public Conversations API. This is not Instagram Direct. The team has to sit inside Stripe to read them. Zapier only fires after money is already refunded.

App Store / Apple

Verba bills iOS through In-App Purchases. Apple owns those refunds, not Stripe. If nobody explains reportaproblem.apple.com, the customer writes in Direct and opens a bank dispute anyway.

Problem

The expensive cases are
refunds, bugs, and policy drift

Apple and Stripe are not the same money

Stripe Dashboard only sees charges that actually ran through Stripe (web, B2B, any non-IAP path). Most iOS revenue never appears there. A human, or a naive bot, who says “I refunded your card” on an App Store charge is lying. Apple may already have refused. The next step is a chargeback.

A chargeback costs more than a refund

Unseen Stripe disputes, failed payments, and refund.failed events sit in Dashboard until someone opens it. You lose the payment, you pay the fee, and you still owe the customer an answer. There is no ticket in Instagram when that happens.

How-to repeats. Real bugs do not.

Fonts, export, teleprompter, captions, SRT, 4K: the site FAQ already answers this. People still DM. Humans retype it at 1am with different wording. A real crash needs device, iOS, and app version. Incomplete reports bounce between support and engineering. Verba ships updates often. Knowledge goes stale between releases.

Refund policy lives in people’s heads

One teammate refunds a yearly plan. Another refuses. Abuse follows the softer answer. There is no encoded window, amount cap, or “Apple vs Stripe” rule. Night coverage is a second salary. Headcount does not scale with featuring or ads.

Share of Instagram vs email vs Stripe vs Apple IAP is unknown until a short call. That mix decides whether Apple notifications matter more than a Stripe email channel.

No, not like Instagram Direct.

Stripe is a billing system with webhooks and a Billing API. Instagram Direct is a messenger with an official inbound and outbound Graph API. Those are different classes of channel. The right build is an adapter, not a copy of Direct.

Refund safety

The agent can return money.
Not however the model feels.

On the platform today, refund always goes to a human handoff. For Verba we open the action, with a hard gate in code, not only in the prompt.

Automatic, if policy matches

  • Stripe Customer plus a charge or invoice, not yet refunded
  • Inside the window (example: 14 days) and at or under the cap (example: $29.99)
  • No open dispute
  • One refund per customer per N days
  • Reason on the whitelist: unused, billing error, crash

Human only (Telegram approve)

  • Amount above the cap, or a yearly plan
  • Repeat request or suspected abuse
  • Customer already used generation limits
  • Apple IAP: the agent does not refund via Stripe
  • Chargeback: dispute process, not a parallel refund

Every create_refund is written to the tenant audit log. Your manager sees charge id, amount, reason. Stripe keys stay in tenant env, never in logs, never in the prompt.

Apple vs Stripe

Most iOS refunds never
show up in Stripe

Verba on the App Store sells In-App Purchases. That money goes through Apple. Stripe only covers payments that actually ran on Stripe.

If Apple billed it

The agent does not call Stripe Refunds. It explains reportaproblem.apple.com, collects Apple ID or email, and tags the thread. Optional phase 4: App Store Server Notifications V2 (CONSUMPTION_REQUEST, REFUND) as another inbound into the same inbox.

If Stripe billed it

The agent looks the customer up, applies policy, refunds or sends a Customer Portal link (cancel renewal, update card), then confirms in DM or email with a 5 to 10 business day statement window.

Without this split the agent promises “refunded to your card” after Apple already said no, and you inherit a dispute. Revenue mix Stripe vs IAP is part of the first call.
