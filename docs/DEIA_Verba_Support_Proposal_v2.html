1. Executive summary

Verba does not need a chatbot bolted onto Instagram. It needs a single support operating system that can answer product questions, collect defect reports, explain Apple versus Stripe billing, execute refunds only when policy allows, and open an engineering ticket when the product is actually broken.

Direct AI Agents already does this for Instagram Direct: isolated tenant, versioned prompts, sandbox, Telegram handoff, audit trail. For Verba we do not fork the product. We provision a dedicated tenant and extend the platform with three capabilities that the current core does not have: a first-class email channel, a Stripe billing adapter (tools + webhooks, not Dashboard chat), and an optional ticket adapter into Linear or Jira Software.

What we recommend

Dedicated tenant. Customer-facing channels: email (official App Store path) and Instagram Direct. Billing truth: Apple IAP and Stripe, treated as separate ledgers. Engineering loop: Linear or Jira issues created by the agent — not a second customer portal.

What we will not sell

Two-way chat inside the Stripe Dashboard. Scraping Stripe’s internal UI. Refunding an App Store charge through Stripe. Replacing Apple’s refund decision. A Jira Service Management customer portal that competes with the agent.

How to buy it

A one-week paid discovery, then a sequenced programme measured in weeks, not days. Indicative full programme: $16,800 one-time and $320/month platform, plus Anthropic usage at cost. Discovery is credited if Verba proceeds.

Decision this document asks for. Approve a 5-day discovery workshop ($1,800, credited). In that week we confirm IAP versus Stripe mix, where receipt replies actually land, which tracker the engineering team already uses, and whether Instagram is a material queue. Commercial options below are then confirmed in a short statement of work.

2. Situation assessment

Verba is an iOS short-form video editor (captions, teleprompter, 4K export, translation, voiceover) sold by Verba Creative Inc., a Delaware corporation. Public store listing: App Store id 6467481048, seller Verba Creative Inc., support address support@verba.digital. The product site claims 100,000+ users. Independent store intelligence (MWM, August 2026) reports 250k+ downloads and a 4.9 store rating. Written reviews are more mixed: subscription friction, export failures, and “the app changed after I paid” appear repeatedly. That gap — high store score, noisy written complaints — is exactly where unattended support burns rating and cash.

The April 2026 privacy policy is explicit: purchases, subscriptions, refunds, and billing support are processed through Apple, Stripe, or other payment providers, including web-to-app checkout. That is the operating fact this proposal is built on. Most iPhone revenue will never appear in Stripe. Most web checkout revenue will never be refundable by Apple.

2.2 Why this is expensive for a five-person company

Fragmented queues

The same person may email support, DM Instagram, tap “request a refund” on a Stripe receipt, and open an Apple refund — with no shared customer record. Humans re-type FAQ answers; real crashes bounce without device / iOS / app version.

Two ledgers, one customer sentence

Listed IAP prices include weekly, monthly, and yearly Pro/Premium (for example yearly $49.99). A reply that says “I refunded your card” on an Apple charge is false. The next step is often a bank dispute.

Policy lives in people’s heads

One teammate refunds a yearly plan; another refuses. Abuse follows the softer answer. Night coverage is a second salary. Headcount does not scale with featuring or ads.

Product ships continuously

Store listing at review time: version 3.66.0 (dual-camera teleprompter). Knowledge that is not tied to release notes goes stale between versions. Incomplete bug reports waste engineering time.

Public company signals used for sizing only: LinkedIn lists Verba as ~5 people, Delaware HQ, Google for Startups backing, modest disclosed funding. These are not audited financials. Discovery will replace them with Verba’s own ticket volume and revenue mix.

5. Channel architecture

Today the Direct AI Agents conversation model is instagram | telegram. Telegram is a team channel, not a customer channel. Verba requires two new customer pipes in the core: email and stripe_event. That is platform work, done once, then available to this tenant.

5.1 Email — first-class customer channel

Inbound: Google Workspace / Gmail API, or inbound parse (Postmark, Mailgun, SendGrid) on support@verba.digital. Threading via In-Reply-To / References. Outbound from the same address so Stripe receipt replies and App Store-originated mail sit in one conversation.

Stripe Public details (support email on receipts and invoices) should point at this same address. That is how “chats from Stripe” become email conversations without a Dashboard API. If Verba later uses a dedicated billing@, it is an alias into the same inbox, not a second product.

Risk to verify in discovery: some Stripe hosted UIs collect a refund request without sending mail. Those requests are Dashboard-only. We will not scrape them. Mitigation: Customer Portal + email path + Instagram, and a weekly Dashboard exception check until Stripe exposes an event (they have not, as of this review).

5.2 Instagram Direct — production channel

Official Meta Graph API, Page token, OAuth, webhook hub. Same runtime as other Direct AI Agents tenants: coalesce, typing, story replies, handoff, smart-trigger, sandbox. Requires an Instagram Business account linked to a Facebook Page and Live-app Advanced Access for instagram_manage_messages if the asset is a third-party business account.

5.3 Stripe — adapter, not a messenger

Three layers, none of which impersonate Dashboard chat:

5.4 Apple — optional, high value if IAP dominates

App Store Server Notifications V2: CONSUMPTION_REQUEST (respond within 12 hours with consumption data and an optional refund preference), REFUND, REFUND_DECLINED. This does not let Verba refund IAP. It lets Verba participate in Apple’s decision and keep the inbox aligned with what Apple already did.

6.1 Refund safety

Automatic, if all gates pass

  • Stripe Customer + charge or PaymentIntent, not yet refunded
  • Inside a written window and at or under a written cap
  • No open dispute on the charge
  • Rate limit: one auto-refund per customer per N days
  • Reason on a whitelist (duplicate, unused, billing error, crash)

Human approve (Telegram)

  • Amount above cap, or yearly plan
  • Repeat request or suspected abuse
  • Material usage of generation limits (if Verba can supply the signal)
  • Anything billed by Apple
  • Open chargeback — dispute process, not a second refund

Every create_refund is written to the tenant audit log (charge id, amount, reason, actor = agent or approver). Keys stay in tenant environment, never in logs, never in the prompt. Refund reason on the Stripe object uses Stripe’s enum (duplicate, fraudulent, requested_by_customer); Verba’s richer reason lives in metadata and the ticket.

7.1 What the adapter actually does

  • Create an issue when the agent classifies a novel defect, with device / iOS / app version / logs / customer language / channel link.
  • Comment when the customer sends a follow-up (deduped by conversation id).
  • Read status so the agent can say “the team has this as VER-142, still open” without leaking internals the customer should not see.
  • Never auto-close a customer thread solely because an engineer hit Done — the agent confirms with the customer.

This mirrors how Direct AI Agents already talks to CRMs: getCrmAdapter + action routing. Ticketing gets the same pattern. HTTP does not live in the conversation orchestrator.

9. Solution design

9.1 What is reused unchanged

  • Isolated tenant provisioning (separate OS user, database, admin domain, process tree).
  • Claude Agent SDK runtime, versioned system prompts, meta-agent (prompt editor), sandbox.
  • Telegram as manager channel: takeover, return, refund approve.
  • Handoff, tags, client profile, audit-minded logging (no secrets in prompts or logs).

9.2 What we add in the platform core

  • Conversation channels email and stripe_event (schema, queue, inbox filters).
  • Support tool pack: Stripe lookup / portal / gated refund / cancel; create_ticket / update_ticket.
  • Policy engine as code (window, cap, allowlist, dispute guard) — prompts cannot bypass it.
  • Ticket adapter interface (Linear, Jira Software).
  • Optional App Store Server Notifications receiver.

9.3 What is tenant configuration, not engineering

  • Support prompt in Verba’s tone, including hard no’s (no fake Stripe refunds on IAP).
  • Knowledge: FAQ, in-app paths, current version caveats, refund policy text.
  • Release-note ingestion process after each App Store version.
  • Sandbox pack: how-to, Apple refund, Stripe duplicate charge, crash with missing version, angry yearly refund.

9.4 Agent mode

Direct AI Agents modes today: sales, leadgen, booking, general. Verba should not run booking or catalogue sales tools. We add support tools on a dedicated support configuration (or a narrow general with sales/booking tools disabled). Refund remains impossible without the policy engine — the same principle as today’s request_handoff for payments, opened only through gated tools.

11. Security, privacy, and controls

  • Isolation. Dedicated tenant: separate OS user, database, admin, processes. Verba transcripts are not visible to other brands.
  • Stripe. Restricted API key (RAK), least privilege: read customers/charges/subscriptions; write refunds and portal sessions only after gates exist. Test mode first. Key in tenant secret store, not in git, not in prompts. IP allow-list if Verba’s Stripe account supports it.
  • Apple. App Store Connect key used only for ASSN and consumption API. No attempt to refund IAP through Stripe.
  • Meta. OAuth Page token. Scopes limited to messaging. No password collection.
  • Mailbox. Domain remains Verba’s. We receive via API or inbound parse; we do not take over the Google account.
  • Personal data. Verba is controller (privacy policy). DEP Software is processor for the tenant. DPA available before live Stripe keys. Support content may include user media metadata — we do not need video binaries in the agent context.
  • Residency / subprocessors. Claude (Anthropic), Meta, Stripe, Apple, optional Linear/Atlassian, email provider. Listed in the DPA.
  • Human override. Takeover freezes the agent on that thread. Refunds above cap cannot execute without Telegram approve.
  • Export / exit. Conversations, customers, tickets references exportable as JSON/CSV. Cancellation does not hold data hostage.
